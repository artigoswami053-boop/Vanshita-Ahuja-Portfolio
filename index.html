Mamabefit/ Health • Fitness • Women's Wellness

Turning a quiet account into one that started moving.

The account

The challenge

The account had reach. It just wasn't translating into enough interaction.

Views and organic interactions were low, follower growth had turned negative, and the account was relying heavily on paid activity.

What I worked on

I reworked the content direction, changed the content pillars and tested themes around postpartum recovery and nutrition myths. Alongside content, I handled the community and worked on generating organic leads.

Before and after

  • Interactions→1,901 → 5,214
  • Monthly views→367K → 503.3K
  • Accounts reached→~100K → 223,037
  • Best post reach→10K → 35K
